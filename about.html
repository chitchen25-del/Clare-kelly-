Practitioner Profile

Bridging Functional Medicine & Advanced Soft-Tissue Therapy

Clare Townsend is the Isle of Man’s premier functional medicine and advanced soft-tissue specialist. With an extraordinary 19 years of dedicated clinical mastery, Clare has pioneered a completely bespoke, dual-modality approach that fundamentally redefines the standard of modern patient care.

She is relentlessly committed to the absolute forefront of medical science, continuously integrating the latest breakthrough clinical research, elite laboratory testing, and highly advanced nutritional protocols into her daily practice. Her unparalleled diagnostic precision allows her to uncover the exact physiological root drivers of chronic illness that standard assessments repeatedly miss.

By seamlessly blending this elite, high-touch functional medicine with exceptionally targeted, hands-on physical therapies, she doesn't merely manage symptoms. Clare permanently resolves complex chronic pain, eradicates systemic inflammation, and profoundly restores her patients' vitality—delivering truly life-changing, lasting results.
