Making An Appointment

What should I expect during my first visit?

A medical history will be taken on the initial visit, and a list of your medications will be needed. We will discuss your goals and perform a postural assessment before beginning treatment.

What should I wear to my session?

The treatment can be done through loose, thin clothing. Please wear something you feel comfortable moving around in.

How many sessions will I need?

Did you know you can turn the volume down on your pain? It won't happen by itself and you need to be ready to work towards it with me. A course of 4 sessions over a period of a month is the normal recommendation for sustainable results.
